Download Instagram stories as MP4

Authentication (required for private / stories)
Stories always require login. Open Instagram in your browser, open DevTools → Application → Cookies → instagram.com, then copy the value of the sessionid cookie and paste it above.
Your session ID is never stored or sent anywhere except to Instagram.

Only public stories or stories visible with your account are downloadable.
